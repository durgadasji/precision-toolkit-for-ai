# Dimensional Frame Language

## What it is

Dimensional Frame Language is a three-stance map of how a describer stands in relation to what they describe, paired with an apparatus that detects, in actual vocabulary, which stance a term is speaking from. The full precise name disambiguates it from the linguistic-frame traditions of Fillmore, Barsalou, Löbner, and Goffman, which it draws on but is not. The three stances are Frame 3, Frame 2, and Frame 1, and the organizing image is person, statue, shadow: a living person, the statue carved from them, and the shadow the person casts, each a further reduction of the one source.

## A note on terms

The canonical vocabulary of this document is operational English. The work has roots in three source traditions, the Advaita and Samkhya material on the philosophical side and the Löbner, Buddhist, and Advaita semantic theories on the linguistic side, and the terms of those traditions are named where a source is being cited. They are not the operative vocabulary. Where a tradition's word is the precise pointer to a source, it appears in that role and no other.

## Genealogy (operative-core, as recorded)

The vocabulary began from a holding-frame idea, keeping one's own definition of a situation against another's. It was run upward into a structural reading, then resolved into a three-stance map with explicit identifications to the source traditions: Frame 3 to the non-dual whole of Advaita, Frame 2 to the conditioned, structured level of Samkhya and the level the best Western philosophy reaches, Frame 1 to the conditioned surface stance that takes the dominant projection for the whole. The linguistic grounding came last, and the comparative work shows it converges with the source traditions' own semantics rather than overlaying them. Arriving late did not make it minor: it added real diagnostic weight, deepening how sharply the frames can be caught in actual text, which has its own section below. Development order is not inheritance order: the linguistics is downstream of the map in origin and load-bearing in operation.

## The three frames (operative-core)

Frame 3 is the Innate Totality, the whole of what a thing is prior to and inclusive of all its expressions. It is pointed toward, never specified. It is, strictly, not a frame at all but the absence of the condition that generates frames; calling it Frame 3 is a naming convenience. The person.

Frame 2 is the level of conditions, configurations, and structure: named arrangements that enable or constrain surface expressions. It is the buildable structural domain, where coordination instruments are made. On the source side it is the conditioned, guna-structured nature of Samkhya, the level the best Western philosophy reaches and does not cross, and the positive attribute-value frame of Löbner semantics. The statue.

Frame 1 is the conditioned, surface-bound stance that takes a vivid projection for the whole truth, mistaking the visible season for the life of the plant. The shadow.

Inheritance runs one direction only, from the Innate Totality through Frame 2 into Frame 1. Building runs downward along that line into structure; the way back toward the Innate Totality is not built but removed, since you cannot compose your way to the source, only strip away all that is not it, which is the ladder treated below. A lower frame cannot assess a higher one, which is a structural fact of the map rather than an imposed prohibition.

## The three capacities (operative-core)

Three capacities run in every perception, in some ratio, and the ratio, not the substrate, determines the frame. Suppression reduces the clarity of what is present. Projection throws a plausible, ready-made object onto the suppressed surface. Perception recognizes the actual conditions. Frame 1 oscillates Projection over Suppression with little Perception; Frame 2 runs Projection grounded by enough Perception to fashion valid structure; Frame 3 runs almost pure Perception.

These three are the operational names for what the source tradition calls the three gunas, read as the working of the conditioned field: Suppression is the veiling power, Projection the projecting power, and Perception the revealing. The rope mistaken for a snake is the canonical instance and the template for the Frame 1 failure: the rope is first veiled, then the snake is projected onto it, which the tradition calls superimposition. Three features of that anatomy are load-bearing beyond the template. The raw perception is incomplete but not wrong, the rope really is there, so Suppression is reduced clarity of something real, not fabrication, and Projection fills the gap. The dependence is one-directional, the snake depends on the rope and the rope does not depend on the snake, which is the grounding for inheritance running one way only. And the resolution is by illumination, not repair, the snake is not removed but vanishes because it was never there, so seeing rightly dissolves the projection rather than patching it, and the verification move the projection forecloses, turning on more light, is exactly the place disconfirmation occupies, which is why disconfirmation is the structural antidote and not a nicety.

What Frame 3 points toward is the no-preference orientation, holding the three capacities without preferring any. It is not the highest capacity; the highest capacity, Perception, is still a capacity, and clinging to its clarity is the Frame 3 failure. The failure structure is nested: the same error recurs at higher abstraction, a preferred direction in Frame 1, a preferred configuration in Frame 2, a preferred position in Frame 3, and the sophistication makes each harder to detect from inside.

One precision for the open Samkhya question: the three capacities are read here as the working of the conditioned field, which is an Advaita reading, and that reading is sourced; the gunas as such are Samkhya. The load-bearing grounding is the Advaita one and is already in hand, with Samkhya standing as the heritage of the three capacities rather than the operative source.

## Transclusion and the two ladders (operative-core and grounding)

Transclusion is the single move the whole map turns on: transcending while constitutively including, with exclusion never permitted during the move. The test is the frame at which the original precision never had to leave. Its prior condition is that differentiation must precede unity, and it has two failure modes, false separation, which is reaching into a part to build a unity, and false merger, which is unity asserted before differentiation completes. Precision and non-harming are one transclusion move seen from two positions, which is why they are one commitment and not two.

The move is bidirectional: composition ascends, finding a broader frame while the lower precision stays fully present, and decomposition descends, generating specific instances that carry the full ground within them. The cascade is the constitutive-containment form, Frame 3 holding Frame 2 holding Frame 1, each readable at full precision without any other stepping back.

The ladder in this work is a specific and easily mistaken thing, so three readings have to be kept apart. The forbidden ladder is the one climbed and stood upon, treating Frame 3 as a position reached and occupied; that is the transcendence-claim failure, and the work guards it with an explicit No-ladder rule. The superimpose-then-rescind teaching ladder, climbed and then abandoned, is closer but still incomplete. The ladder as this work means it is the removal process: the way back toward Frame 3 is taken by stripping away all that is not Frame 3 until only Frame 3 remains, not by building. The Frame 3 actor designs and walks the full structure in both directions, building downward into Frame 2 structure and removing upward toward the source, and that design is their fundamental responsibility. Designing and walking it is the positive duty whose refusal is the single Frame 3 failure, so the No-ladder guard and this ladder are complementary, the guard naming the failure and the ladder naming the duty.

Two disciplines keep it precise, and the second answers the charge of self-sealing rather than conceding it. First, the building stays Frame 2: you cannot compose your way up to the source, so what is built reaches no higher than Frame 2, and the Decomposition Designer, the tool that mechanizes the building and the grading, has no Frame 3 check because Frame 3 cannot be language. Second, the way up is removal, and removal is exactly why the position is not self-sealing. Frame 3 is never posited as a claim to be taken on trust; it is what remains when all that is not it has been removed, and each removal is an ordinary Frame 2 check, this is not it, strip it, that an outside party can verify. Climbing the ladder is the repeated practice of that removal, and it is externally legible step by step, by what it discards and by the round-trip soundness of what it then builds. The one thing removal leaves to first-person observation is the content of the residue, whether what remains reads as a positive ground or as an emptiness, which is the divergence between the affirmative and the exclusion traditions and the one commitment this work makes; that is traced by its fruits, not immunized by design. So the ladder is the removal, the climbing is the practice, the practice is verifiable, and the orientation it cultivates over time is what lets the next building be done without the preferred direction or configuration that is the Frame 2 failure, a developmental spiral in which a tool can carry the building but never the practice. ## The inter-frame operation: selective deletion (grounding)

The operation that moves between frames is a type-shift. Take a term whose primary meaning carries qualifiers that make it contradictory at the target level, discard the incompatible qualifiers, keep the invariant residue, and assert the result at the new level. This is selective deletion: the source tradition names it bhaga-tyaga-lakshana, the operation that lets "that thou art" function as identity by stripping the cosmic qualifiers from "that" and the individual qualifiers from "thou" and keeping the bare existence-awareness common to both. In formal semantics the same operation is coercion, the type-shift triggered when ordinary composition is blocked, which the linguistic analysis credited as the one genuinely generative move in the apparatus. They are the same operation reached from opposite ends, an independent convergence, not a borrowing. The trigger is a compositional conflict; the result is a coherent structure at the new level. This is the named formal account of how the frames relate.

## The three-tradition semantic grounding (grounding)

Three semantic traditions constitute meaning three ways, and Dimensional Frame Language sits at their synthesis. Löbner frames build meaning positively, by attribute-value unification and cascades that add levels; this is the Frame 2 compositional machinery. Buddhist exclusion semantics, apoha, builds meaning negatively, a term meaning the contrast class of what it is not, with no positive residue; this is the structure of stripping by negation. Advaita indication, lakshana, builds meaning by selective deletion, decomposing a prior structure, discarding incompatible parts, keeping the invariant residue; this is the ascent toward the Innate Totality. The three define a space, and the frames occupy a precise, defensible point in it.

The point fixes one falsifiable commitment. The exclusion and indication traditions agree on stripping by negation but diverge on what the stripping reaches: exclusion reaches an empty contrast class, indication reaches a positive, self-evident ground. Dimensional Frame Language takes the second side: Frame 3 is a positive ground, the Innate Totality, not an emptiness. That is a choice, not a neutral observation, and it is the load-bearing commitment of the whole map.

## The three methods and their failure modes (grounding, and a discipline)

The three traditions are also three methods for handling the source, and each has a characteristic failure that has to be named, because this work uses the third and must keep the first two from collapsing into it.

Positive assertion, the building method, reaches the highest structure it can build and stops. Its failure is to mistake that ceiling for the source and to keep asserting positively past the point where assertion can reach, since you cannot compose your way to the source. This is the step Western philosophy never closes: it arrives at the doorstep, the recognition that the source is a non-relational whole the intellect cannot deliver, and does not cross, because crossing is removal and removal is not an assertion. The sharper form of the failure is smuggling, a positive assertion dressed as a neutral or minimal one, carrying content it denies carrying.

Pure negation, the exclusion method, removes all the way down and lets nothing remain. Its failure is the empty terminus, and the regress that the contrast class needs a prior sorting it cannot itself supply. It is honest that nothing positive is left, which is its strength as a semantics and its limit as a path, because there is no ground to stand the next building on.

Removal, the method this work uses, strips away all that is not the source until the source remains, and it has three things of its own to guard. The first and most dangerous is incomplete removal that smuggles a positive back in while denying it. The clean shape is a stance that attempts a negation, stops short, substitutes a positive in the cleared space, and denies that it has, the denial being the concealment that makes the smuggled positive invisible. A worldview that presents itself as merely the removal of a claim, while quietly carrying and defending a full positive account it disavows holding, has made exactly this move. This work makes its one positive commitment, the residue read as a ground rather than an emptiness, in the open and traced by its fruits, precisely so that it is not a smuggled positive of that kind. The second is over-accommodation, the removal apparatus retracting any objection as provisional. The third is not a failure but a cost: removal is logical, but completing it is so hard that it can only be practiced, never finished by a procedure.

That cost fixes what an instrument can and cannot do. An automated tool, the Decomposition Designer with the three-capacity grading, can run the logical removal and grade each element up to the best Frame 2 it can reach, the clear and whole-oriented one. It cannot complete the removal into Frame 3, because the final step is not a grade or a logical move but a practice, and the three capacities are themselves Frame 2 grades, so grading by them reaches their own ceiling and not past it. An instrument carries the removal to the Frame 2 ceiling; a practitioner completes it, asymptotically, through a practice no instrument performs.

## The failure-mode study (operative-core, the most developed part)

This is the deepest and most mature body of the work, specified at two layers that were deliberately cross-checked: the five-class vocabulary typology, which says where in a translation the inheritance chain breaks, and the three-capacity configuration mapping, which says what ratio of Suppression, Projection, and Perception produces the break. The asymmetry across frames is principled, not a gap.

Frame 1 has four failure configurations, all sharing precision structurally absent because Projection is fashioning from a suppressed surface: structural blindness, inherited vocabulary taken as complete; arrested aspiration, a Frame 2 modifier on a Frame 1 head noun where the specification never completes; affective substitution, values language standing in for structural action; and strategic avoidance, Frame 3 language grabbed to bypass a precision requirement.

Frame 2 has three, all sharing non-harming compromised because perceptual clarity has misattached, fastening onto the vocabulary or the instrument rather than looking through it at the conditions and the cost-bearing parties: vocabulary performing structure, correct terms with no conditions beneath them; reaching past the frame, Frame 3 claimed as an occupiable level; and precision imposition, specifications so mutually elaborate that no action satisfies them while conformance stays demonstrable.

Frame 3 has exactly one: full clarity present and compositional movement refused, the seer treating the seeing as a sufficient response and withholding it from the structural commitment it should produce. On the source side this is cessation of activity presented as genuine freedom from binding action, the error that traditional scholarship diagnoses precisely against the correct reading, in which the realized actor keeps acting for the welfare of the structural whole. The mode is held under the place-do-not-characterize discipline, and the typology classes that involve Frame 3 language are routed as Frame 1 or Frame 2 failures, because Frame 3 itself does nothing in them.

One cross-cutting diagnostic belongs with these, because it wears the look of the move toward the source while doing the opposite. The half-removal is a negation that presents as clearing away, claims to be only denying or to be the neutral and minimal position, but stops short of completing the removal and installs a positive claim in the cleared space, then denies that anything was installed. Its detectable signature is the denial itself: the stance defends a substantive positive while insisting it holds none, and treats the request to examine that positive as out of bounds. It appears in both lower registers, since the smuggled positive can be a Frame 1 surface claim or a Frame 2 structural one, and it is the method-level failure of incomplete removal seen at the frame level. The guard is the discipline this work holds for itself: any positive that survives a removal is carried in the open and traced by its fruits, never left as a residue the framing disavows.

## The apparatus (operative-core, with one drafted part)

The grammar runs three procedures in order: should this Frame 1 term be replaced here, is the Frame 2 replacement strong enough, and is the replaced content actually functioning. The first two are mature. The third, the Frame 2 functioning check, is derived from first principles but not yet field-tested The grammar carries the watchlist, the canonical table of recurring Frame 1 terms mapped to Frame 2 replacements with strengthened forms and a procedure for each, and the strengthening typology behind it, which adds the suppressed mechanism back when a correct replacement comes out weaker than the term it replaced. The construction-grammar tests are drafted but not yet inserted. Selective deletion belongs here as a named operation alongside the grammar.

## The modern frame's diagnostic contribution (operative-core, recently deepened)

The philosophical root supplies what the frames are; the modern frame, the formal-semantic and construction-grammar work, supplies how sharply they can be caught in actual text, and it added real diagnostic weight rather than restating the philosophy in other words. Three contributions are operative. The construction-grammar tests, blank-filling, subject, and frozen-versus-open, detect whether the structural content sits in the construction or only in the vocabulary, which is the difference between a real Frame 2 specification and a Frame 1 term wearing Frame 2 clothes. The concept-type coercion test detects when a term has been pushed out of its native type, which is the surface trace of an inter-frame move made or smuggled. And the finding that the structural content is in the construction, not the vocabulary, lifts the whole apparatus from a word-level watchlist toward construction-level diagnosis. These are not decoration on the philosophy. They are the operational deepening of the diagnosis, and because the coercion move and the removal operation are the same type-shift, the formal machinery can be run as a detector for exactly the moves the philosophy names. This deepening is recent and is to be built out with the backing the comparative work now supplies, not folded away as a late detail. The next step is to insert the construction-grammar tests into the grammar and add the coercion test.

## Relation to PFDS: twin articulations of one ground

The Precision-First Design Standard and Dimensional Frame Language rest on the same ground: contact with the Innate Totality, and the transclusion move that holds precision and non-harming as one. They articulate it differently. PFDS articulates it structurally, as the precision floor and ceiling derived from one commitment. Dimensional Frame Language articulates it as the three-stance map, the three-capacity account of how the move is made, and the inter-frame semantics of how meaning ascends and descends. Neither inherits its content from the other; both express the root. So Dimensional Frame Language is not a sibling precision instrument beside PFDS, and not a second diagnostic engine peer to it. It is the articulation of the ground that PFDS's central claim, that precision is contact with the Innate Totality rather than a preferred surface expression, silently stands on. The earlier readings, sibling instrument and second engine, were scoped to the linguistic apparatus alone and are superseded. (Ratified 2026-06-11.)

## Vulnerabilities, answered and open

The Frame 3 commitment is often charged with three vulnerabilities, and the removal method answers the first two rather than conceding them. Self-sealing, the charge that Frame 3 is a non-falsifiable positive claim, does not hold here, because Frame 3 is not posited: it is reached by removing all that is not it, each removal is an ordinary verifiable check, and the practice is therefore externally legible step by step and by its fruits, not immunized by design. Residue indeterminacy, the charge that stripping yields a positive ground only if the metaphysics is granted, narrows to one first-person observation, whether what remains reads as a positive ground or as an emptiness, which is the single commitment this work makes and is traced by its fruits rather than asserted. The one genuinely open edge is over-accommodation: the superimpose-then-rescind structure can in principle absorb objections as provisional teachings to be retracted, and that is a discipline to watch rather than a closed question. Separately, some deeper Frame 3 material is internally coherent but externally unchecked, and it is held back until a reader who does not already hold the frame has checked it.

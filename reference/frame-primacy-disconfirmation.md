# Frame-primacy disconfirmation

Status: Public release version (2026-06-12). Originally Created 2026-06-03. The horizontal complement to the Dimensional Frame Language diagnostic, placed with the Dimensional Frame Language discipline.

## The method

Frame-primacy disconfirmation: use a sincerely held rival frame as the probe whose disagreements localize non-minimal configurations and whose convergences mark the minimal basis, as the horizontal complement to Dimensional Frame Language's vertical placement, with the whole operation staying in Frame 2 and Frame 3 entering only as the orientation that names a configuration non-minimal.

## Why a held frame is the precise instrument

A frame is a configuration with a preferred space, and its adversarial surface is exactly the boundary of that preference, the region it cannot assess from inside. A sincerely held rival frame is therefore a more precise adversary than a generic critic, because it attacks exactly where its configuration differs rather than anywhere. Convergence between two independently built frames marks something close to a minimal basis, because both decompose to it; divergence marks a non-minimal configuration, a preference mistaken for a primitive, in one or both. The output is a better composition, the smaller basis both frames reduce into, not a verdict on which frame is right.

## Level discipline

The operation is Frame 2 throughout: comparing configurations, finding the minimal-basis composition, decomposing the rivals into it. It does not reach or characterize Frame 3, which is practiced into, not built; you cannot compose your way to the source. Frame 3 enters only as orientation, the no-preferred-expression stance that flags a configuration as non-minimal. The minimal basis the method produces is optimal Frame 2 (precise composition from a minimal basis, every form losslessly decomposable back to it), the asymptotic target the corpus always approaches and never reaches. Naming the result a Frame 3 move is a recurring looseness to guard against; the moves are optimal Frame 2.

## The two halves of the diagnostic

Dimensional Frame Language as a diagnostic now has a vertical half and a horizontal half. Vertical, the original DFL-as-diagnostic: place a frame at its level and detect the Frame 3 failure mode as the absence of the composition genuine clear-seeing would produce. Horizontal, this method: diagnose, within Frame 2, how close a configuration is to optimal, by probing it with a rival held frame, and generate the reduction. The first worked example placed a curation frame held by an external reviewer at Frame 2 (vertical) and then used that held frame to surface CRAFT's own non-minimal configurations and produce the smaller basis both share, claimant-uncontrollable overturning (horizontal).

## The Frame 1 companion obligation

This method serves optimal Frame 2. Optimal Frame 1 is a co-equal obligation, not an afterthought, and where Frame 1 is the right frame it must be done at highest sharpness. Optimal Frame 1 is three things: using Frame 1 where it is actually right, in settled and knowable territory where flat prescription is correct and Frame 2 would over-engineer; producing the best possible flat projection of Frame 2 content for the Frame 1 reader, the dimensional-degradation obligation, which is also the general-reader register of the three-reader standard; and decomposing Frame 1 vocabulary into the Frame 2 structure it conceals, the translation. The shared engine for both frames is precise composition and decomposition. A standing guard follows: do not Frame-2 settled territory, because over-engineering a knowable flat prescription is its own failure, the mirror of leaving a configuration non-minimal.

## The sharpness precondition

The method is only as trustworthy as the sharpness of the frames it compares, and this is a precondition, not an optional refinement. Convergence and divergence are the signal: convergence marks the minimal basis, divergence marks a non-minimal configuration. But a frame held below its sharpest opens a second source of divergence that is noise, a disagreement that is an artifact of holding the frame sloppily rather than a real configuration-preference. So a divergence has two possible sources and the method must distinguish them: a genuine configuration-preference (signal) or insufficient sharpness in one frame (noise). The only suppressant for the second is to hold every frame in the comparison, the probed structure and the rival both, at its sharpest in its own right register before and during the run: optimal Frame 1 where Frame 1 is the right frame, minimal-basis optimal Frame 2 where Frame 2 is. This is what makes optimal Frame 1 a must rather than a preference: run a Frame 1 frame at less than optimal Frame 1 and every divergence it produces is suspect, because the diagnostic is reading its own slack as the rival's configuration.

The discipline is therefore iterative, because a frame cannot fully verify its own sharpness from inside, the Flatland fact turned on itself. Sharpen each frame as far as the DFL diagnostic and composition to a minimal basis allow, run the comparison, and treat each divergence as suspect until residual slack in one frame has been ruled out as its source. Confirming the rival frame at its sharpest, with the person who holds it, is part of this precondition and not courtesy: a divergence found against a reconstruction of a frame can be an artifact of the reconstruction rather than a real difference. Convergence is more robust, surviving even a roughly held rival; divergence needs the sharp frame to count as signal.

## What the CRAFT grind added to the method (2026-06-03)

The CRAFT conditional-invariant grind (three disconfirmation rounds, ratified) sharpened the method in four ways.

The mis-located-trigger check. The recurring error across the grind was an invariant keyed to a property that travels with the real triggering property rather than the one that necessitates the form. The check, now a standing method element: for every invariant, find the property that necessitates the form, and construct a case where the candidate trigger holds but the form is not needed, and the reverse. For a requirement claimed universal, the same check becomes a hidden-trigger hunt: construct a chain where it does not apply, which reveals the property it secretly depended on. Run against the five CRAFT universals, this left only two standing (non-vacuity and consequence-calibration) and exposed an empirical-versus-constructive antecedent the slate had not carried.

The independence ladder. Frames are not the only probe, and rival-frame prose is the most correlated one. The ladder, most correlated first: a single in-context pass; independent rival frames in prose; maximally orthogonal frames including ones from outside the corpus; and the self-administered hard plates, namely constructed counterexamples (empirical, the most decisive adversary in the grind) and full primary-literature reads (grounding against real external text rather than the model's guess at a frame). A live external expert is the asymptote, reached for only on the irreducible residue, and a proof-assistant formalization is a deterministic plate to be run when the tooling is present. The discipline is to climb as far as the available plates allow before claiming an expert residue, which keeps that residue small and honest.

The entailment test, and its limit. The grind's load-bearing admission test was that a requirement is an invariant in parametric form only where the declared structural property necessitates the form, not merely where the form is a function of the property, which is too weak a schema since almost any conditional satisfies it. But the test is not an objective check: whether a property necessitates a form is relative to how the property and form are individuated, so it is salvageable only as a relative discipline, under a declared individuation (a no-trigger-import rule barring a property from naming its own form, plus a declared grain and a declared dimension along which grain is taken) and a declared goal background. This is the method's own cannot-ground-its-own-frame limit appearing in use.

Declaration over read-off, and where it actually routes (tested 2026-06-03 against the full PFDS standard, version 2.3.0). The deepest convergence of the grind was that robust invariants key to declared and justified properties of the process, the parties, and the frame, never to a property read off the object or assumed canonical, with the audit checking the declaration's coherence. Routing this was itself tested rather than asserted. The standard shows it is not a candidate for a new PFDS principle, because it is already PFDS: Corollary 1 (operational definition, a property must be determinable by an independent observer from the definition and observable evidence, not read off through privileged or intuitive access), reinforced by Corollary 9 (a reading's meaning is relative to a declared baseline, not read off the signal) and Corollary 10 (the object must be declared as criterion or construct, with a declared nomological network for constructs). CRAFT's object-directness invariant is Corollary 10 almost verbatim. So the declaration discipline is the inheritance of PFDS Corollaries 1, 9, and 10, not a method-local or CRAFT-native principle, and routing it up as new would have conflated chronological discovery (surfaced in the grind) with structural origin (PFDS, upstream), the precision failure PFDS's own inheritance rule names. The genuine upward candidate is narrower: the grind sharpened Corollary 10, splitting its criterion-construct binary into a three-valued object typology (criterion, causal-formative construct, composite) and adding an empirical-versus-constructive scope antecedent, since constructive chains void the directness, drift, and envelope requirements. Whether that refinement is folded into PFDS is Durgadas's normative call.

## What the boundary work added to the method (2026-06-04)

A later session, working from the frozen state of the CRAFT grind, found where the method crosses from reduction into ruin and built the guard the method had been missing. The grind had run past its own completion and oscillated rather than converged; this is the response.

The one-step cliff and the inverted signal. The method is a reduction: it removes non-minimal configurations until only the minimal basis remains, and while it is reducing it is self-limiting, because each round leaves less to cut and the rounds find less, then nothing. Past the minimal basis the operation inverts on a single step. There is no floor below the basis, so a further round no longer removes a real non-minimal configuration; it manufactures divergences against a sound result, and because the probe is correlated with the result (see the ladder above) each manufactured divergence invites a fix that gives the next round a fresh crack. The failure is fast, not gradual, and it carries an inverted signal: before the basis, finding a divergence means there is excess to cut, which is progress; after the basis, finding a divergence means the probe is fabricating, which is the onset of ruin. The two feel identical from inside, so the stop cannot be the runner's sense that there is still something to fix. This is distinct from the sharpness precondition above: sharpness decides whether a given divergence is real, and the boundary decides whether the run is finished.

The dry round as the exit. Name the completion so it can be enforced: the dry round is the first round that introduces no new independent surface and breaks nothing further, and it is the boundary. At the dry round the run is over; emit the residue and route it outward. The rule that holds the line is one sentence: no new round without a new independent surface. A divergence having been produced is not a reason to continue, because a probe run past the basis always produces one.

The surface-novelty test and its red line. The test that decides whether a round counts is not whether its divergence is real, which is the judgment the boundary fools, but whether the round brought a new independent surface, drawn from the high rungs of the ladder above. That question is close to mechanical: require each round to name the new surface it used, and if it cannot, the round is invalid and the run is over. The red line: the test may never be the runner's sense of improvement, and the surface that counts is never one more pass of the most correlated probe. A fan-out of rival frames generated from one mind is one surface wearing several costumes, the two-can-be-matched-curves failure raised a level, and its apparent convergence certifies nothing.

The ratchet, the revert, and the registry. Because text is recoverable where a ground plate is not, snapshot the state before each round, keep the round only if it named a new independent surface that broke something, and otherwise revert to the last surface-anchored state. Write each stage to an append-only registry carrying its own identifier, its parent, the surface it introduced, the verdict, and the residue, so reverts branch the run into a tree, the stop becomes a fact a deterministic gate can read rather than a feeling, and the run is auditable from outside. The registry raises legibility and auditability, not correctness; a traceable fooled run is still fooled.

Positioning against the literature. This boundary account was reasoned from the metrology lineage and converges with an active 2024 to 2026 machine-learning literature that reached the same structures independently, which by the method's own logic is corroboration rather than priority. That single-model self-critique degrades a correct result and inverts past an optimal point is established (Huang et al., Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet, ICLR 2024; Pan et al., Spontaneous Reward Hacking in Iterative Self-Refinement, 2024; Liu and Wang, Answer Convergence as a Signal for Early Stopping, 2025; Zhou et al., When More Thinking Hurts, 2026). That same-base-model critics share biases so their agreement does not certify correctness is established (Kim et al., Correlated Errors in Large Language Models, ICML 2025; Zhang et al., Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate, 2025). The narrow contribution the search did not find already assembled is two-fold: the three-plate argument used as the explicit justification for the independence requirement, and the novel-content stopping rule wired as a gate over an immutable, revert-branching registry. A fuller treatment is held in the disconfirmation-construct design notes.

## Status

First real uses: the first worked run, then the CRAFT conditional-invariant grind (three rounds, ratified 2026-06-03) and the CRAFT universals check. The method is strengthened but still provisional, and is itself a candidate evaluation primitive to be tested and sharpened by further use. The boundary work (2026-06-04) added the completion guard the method lacked, the dry-round exit and the surface-novelty gate and the ratchet and registry, and a prior-art check found the failure mode and the early-stopping remedy already established in the 2024 to 2026 literature while two narrow framings remain the contribution. The surviving CRAFT universals have now faced the check; consequence-calibration and non-vacuity survived. The method's own residue is the entailment-test limit above (the individuation regress) and the owed proof-assistant formalization of the fibration claim.

## The boundary: metrology is one strand, not the basis

Precision is a stance carried into coordination and evaluation from the fields that developed it, and metrology is one of those fields, not the whole. Stating metrology as the basis would be a preferred configuration mistaken for the universal, the failure the precision discipline cuts everywhere else. Other fields contribute their own strands, linguistics, institutional analysis, forecasting, and the contemplative traditions among them. Metrology's strand is the precision and measurement problems specifically: how to build a true reference, a unit, a composition, and a traceable chain when precision must be built rather than assumed.

## Why the metrological principle is constitutive, not analogy

The metrological principle is not borrowed as resemblance. By the cross-domain-synthesis method, it is an independently convergent vantage on the same coordination-structural reality. Physical measurement solved the problem of building precision without a pre-existing reference; coordination and evaluation face that same problem; so the metrological solution is at once evidence of convergence and a reusable solution. The discoveries here are the metrological solution reinstantiated where the substrate is coordination rather than steel, because the problem is the same problem.

## The metrology facet's constitutive map

The metrological problem in one line: build a true reference when there is no pre-existing reference to build it from. The physical solutions, the four preconditions plus the bootstrap, reinstantiate as follows:

- The traceability chain reinstates as CRAFT's chains. CRAFT, Chains Reveal Attested Falsifiable Truth, is a traceability chain for a claim: every claim traceable back through the evaluation chain to its decision context, the way every physical measurement is traceable to the standard. It is not like a traceability chain; it is one, carrying truth instead of length.
- Gauge blocks, and the principle that precision does not transfer between layers automatically, reinstate as PFDS composition. Gauge blocks build any dimension by wringing standards together, and the no-automatic-transfer point is already in PFDS as the gauge-block principle. PFDS composition from a minimal basis, every form a configuration of primitives losslessly decomposable, is that move.
- The standard unit reinstates as the operational definition, the declared validity basis of Condition 1, the agreed reference against which a reading counts as the thing it claims to be.
- Precision-first reinstates as PFDS itself. The metrologist's stance, get the reference right before you measure, is precision-first design, get the specification precise before the system operates.
- The three-plate method, the surface-plate bootstrap, reinstates as frame-primacy disconfirmation. The method, which Henry Maudslay originated around 1800 and Whitworth described and refined in his 1840 paper, grinds three imperfect surfaces against each other in all pairings until the only plane common to all three is the true flat, no pre-existing reference required. Frame-primacy disconfirmation grinds three or more independent held frames against each other until the only shared minimal basis is the one all of them reduce into, which is a very good Frame 2 and not a Frame 3 verdict: the method establishes the verified shared floor, never the worth or the source, the limit the frame application states in full. PFDS itself was bootstrapped this way: precision and non-harming mutually corrected until they converged on the standard that holds both, no external authority supplying the reference.

## The facet generating discovery

Precision keeps producing discovery for a structural reason: each round of precision extends the measurement surface, and each extension reveals what the surface now makes visible. The precision work of treating frames as measurement instruments extended the measurement surface and revealed: frame-primacy disconfirmation, the three-plate method applied to frames, with its requirement of three rather than two independent frames because two surfaces can be complementary curves that match without either being flat; the sharper Occam's razor, the measurement criterion for what is excess, where an entity is excess if a sharp rival frame exposes it as a preferred configuration and the lossless-reconstruction constraint forecloses over-cutting, which makes it sharper than classic Occam by supplying a test, defining necessity as lossless reconstruction, and never amputating real structure; the sharpness precondition, that the razor is only as sharp as the frames and they must be honed before cutting; and the closing of the open PoC Three-Plate Question, since the corpus's disconfirmation earns genuine three-plate epistemic standing exactly when run as three or more independent held frames mutually ground.
